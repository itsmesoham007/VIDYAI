- **Core Concepts & Learning Outcomes**: Inside-Outside, Bigger-Smaller, Top-Bottom, Shapes around us

### Comprehensive Study Notes: Shapes and Space

In GSEB Standard 1 Mathematics, Chapter 1 investigates: **Inside-Outside, Bigger-Smaller, Top-Bottom, Shapes around us**.
