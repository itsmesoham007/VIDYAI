## Chapter 1: Accounting for Share Capital
- **Syllabus Identifier**: GSEB-ENG-STD12-ELE-CH01
- **Board**: GSEB (English Medium)
- **Standard**: 12
- **Stream**: Commerce
- **Subject**: Elements of Accounts Part 2
- **Core Concepts & Learning Outcomes**: Issue of shares at par, premium; over-subscription (pro-rata allotment), calls in arrears, forfeiture and reissue of shares, Capital Reserve

### Comprehensive Study Notes: Accounting for Share Capital

In GSEB Standard 12 Elements of Accounts Part 2, Chapter 1 investigates: **Issue of shares at par, premium; over-subscription (pro-rata allotment), calls in arrears, forfeiture and reissue of shares, Capital Reserve**.

#### Core Principles & Educational Foundation:
1. **Conceptual Definition**: Students explore the foundational definitions, scientific/historical principles, and logical frameworks governing Accounting for Share Capital.
2. **Detailed Curriculum Scope**: Focuses strictly on the prescribed Gujarat Board GSSTB English Medium syllabus. Key topics include: Issue of shares at par, premium; over-subscription (pro-rata allotment), calls in arrears, forfeiture and reissue of shares, Capital Reserve.
3. **Practical & Exam Application**: Concepts are linked to everyday life in Gujarat, solved textbook examples, diagrams, and Board exam question formats.


### GSEB Board Examination Focus & High-Yield Q&A:

**GSEB 2-Mark Question**: State the main learning outcome of Standard 12 Elements of Accounts Part 2 Chapter 1 (Accounting for Share Capital).
- **Model Answer**: The chapter establishes core concepts: Issue of shares at par, premium; over-subscription (pro-rata allotment), calls in arrears, forfeiture and reissue of shares, Capital Reserve, preparing students for Board assessments with accurate definitions and problem-solving steps.
