## Chapter 1: Index Numbers
- **Syllabus Identifier**: GSEB-ENG-STD12-STA-CH01
- **Board**: GSEB (English Medium)
- **Standard**: 12
- **Stream**: Commerce
- **Subject**: Statistics Part 1
- **Core Concepts & Learning Outcomes**: Base year selection (fixed vs chain base), Laspeyres, Paasche, and Fisher ideal index numbers, cost of living index number

### Comprehensive Study Notes: Index Numbers

In GSEB Standard 12 Statistics Part 1, Chapter 1 investigates: **Base year selection (fixed vs chain base), Laspeyres, Paasche, and Fisher ideal index numbers, cost of living index number**.

1. **Conceptual Definition**: Students explore the foundational definitions, scientific/historical principles, and logical frameworks governing Index Numbers.

3. **Practical & Exam Application**: Concepts are linked to everyday life in Gujarat, solved textbook examples, diagrams, and Board exam question formats.


### GSEB Board Examination Focus & High-Yield Q&A:

- **Model Answer**: The chapter establishes core concepts: Base year selection (fixed vs chain base), Laspeyres, Paasche, and Fisher ideal index numbers, cost of living index number, preparing students for Board assessments with accurate definitions and problem-solving steps.
