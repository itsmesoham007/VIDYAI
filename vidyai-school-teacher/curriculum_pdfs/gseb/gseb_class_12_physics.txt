## Chapter 1: Electric Charges and Fields
- **Syllabus Identifier**: GSEB-ENG-STD12-PHY-CH01
- **Board**: GSEB (English Medium)
- **Standard**: 12
- **Subject**: Physics
- **Core Concepts & Learning Outcomes**: Coulomb's Law: F = (1/4pi eps0)*(q1*q2/r^2), Gauss's Law: Phi = oint E.dA = Q_enc/eps0, Field of line charge E = lambda/(2pi eps0 r), infinite plane sheet E = sigma/(2eps0)

### Comprehensive Study Notes: Electrostatics, Coulomb's Law, and Gauss's Law

#### 1. Coulomb's Law:
The electrostatic force between two stationary point charges $q_1$ and $q_2$ in vacuum separated by distance $r$ is:
$$F = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}$$
where $\varepsilon_0 = 8.854 \times 10^{-12} \text{ C}^2\text{ N}^{-1}\text{ m}^{-2}$ is the permittivity of free space, and $\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \approx 9 \times 10^9 \text{ N m}^2\text{ C}^{-2}$.

#### 2. Gauss's Law:
The total electric flux $\Phi$ through any closed surface enclosing a net charge $Q_{\text{enclosed}}$ is:
$$\Phi = \oint \vec{E} \cdot d\vec{A} = \frac{Q_{\text{enclosed}}}{\varepsilon_0}$$
- **Electric field of infinitely long charged wire**: $E = \frac{\lambda}{2\pi\varepsilon_0 r}$.
- **Electric field of infinite uniformly charged sheet**: $E = \frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0}$.


### GSEB Board Examination Focus & High-Yield Q&A:

**GSEB 3-Mark Question**: State Coulomb's Law in vector form and define the SI unit of electric charge.
- **Model Answer**: In vacuum: $\vec{F}_{12} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2} \hat{r}_{21}$. One Coulomb is that charge which, when placed at a distance of 1 meter from an identical charge in vacuum, repels it with a force of $9 \times 10^9 \text{ N}$.
