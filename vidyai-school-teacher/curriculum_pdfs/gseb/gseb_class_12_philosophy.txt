## Chapter 1: Theories of Knowledge and Truth (Epistemology)
- **Syllabus Identifier**: GSEB-ENG-STD12-PHI-CH01
- **Board**: GSEB (English Medium)
- **Standard**: 12
- **Stream**: Arts
- **Subject**: Philosophy
- **Core Concepts & Learning Outcomes**: Pramana shastra (Pratyaksha, Anumana, Upamana, Shabda), Correspondence, Coherence, Pragmatic truth theories

### Comprehensive Study Notes: Theories of Knowledge and Truth (Epistemology)

In GSEB Standard 12 Philosophy, Chapter 1 investigates: **Pramana shastra (Pratyaksha, Anumana, Upamana, Shabda), Correspondence, Coherence, Pragmatic truth theories**.

#### Core Principles & Educational Foundation:
1. **Conceptual Definition**: Students explore the foundational definitions, scientific/historical principles, and logical frameworks governing Theories of Knowledge and Truth (Epistemology).
2. **Detailed Curriculum Scope**: Focuses strictly on the prescribed Gujarat Board GSSTB English Medium syllabus. Key topics include: Pramana shastra (Pratyaksha, Anumana, Upamana, Shabda), Correspondence, Coherence, Pragmatic truth theories.
3. **Practical & Exam Application**: Concepts are linked to everyday life in Gujarat, solved textbook examples, diagrams, and Board exam question formats.


### GSEB Board Examination Focus & High-Yield Q&A:

**GSEB 2-Mark Question**: State the main learning outcome of Standard 12 Philosophy Chapter 1 (Theories of Knowledge and Truth (Epistemology)).
- **Model Answer**: The chapter establishes core concepts: Pramana shastra (Pratyaksha, Anumana, Upamana, Shabda), Correspondence, Coherence, Pragmatic truth theories, preparing students for Board assessments with accurate definitions and problem-solving steps.
