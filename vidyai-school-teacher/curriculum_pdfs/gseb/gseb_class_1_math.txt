## Chapter 1: Shapes and Space
- **Syllabus Identifier**: GSEB-ENG-STD01-MAT-CH01

- **Standard**: 1
- **Subject**: Mathematics
- **Core Concepts & Learning Outcomes**: Inside-Outside, Bigger-Smaller, Top-Bottom, Nearer-Farther, Shapes around us (Circle, Square, Triangle)

### Comprehensive Study Notes: Shapes and Space

In GSEB Standard 1 Mathematics, Chapter 1 investigates: **Inside-Outside, Bigger-Smaller, Top-Bottom, Nearer-Farther, Shapes around us (Circle, Square, Triangle)**.

1. **Conceptual Definition**: Students explore the foundational definitions, scientific/historical principles, and logical frameworks governing Shapes and Space.
2. **Detailed Curriculum Scope**: Focuses strictly on the prescribed Gujarat Board GSSTB English Medium syllabus. Key topics include: Inside-Outside, Bigger-Smaller, Top-Bottom, Nearer-Farther, Shapes around us (Circle, Square, Triangle).

### GSEB Board Examination Focus & High-Yield Q&A:
